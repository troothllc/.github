### One public, machine-readable record for every company, read by people and the AI assistants they use.

Trooth operates the Trooth Network. A company publishes its record once, free, and buyers read it with no login instead of sending another questionnaire. Every item shows where it came from and when it was last read. Trooth witnesses and dates facts. It does not grade, rate or rank anyone.
