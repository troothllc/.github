# Getting help

## A question about using Trooth

Write to **hello@trooth.co** or use the form at [trooth.co/contact](https://trooth.co/contact). We reply within two business days.

The documentation is at [trooth.co/docs](https://trooth.co/docs): the [API reference](https://trooth.co/docs/api), the [MCP connector for AI assistants](https://trooth.co/docs/agents), the command-line reader (`npx trooth check <domain>`), and the [public keys](https://trooth.co/verify/keys) for checking a Trooth signature yourself. Service status is at [trooth.co/status](https://trooth.co/status).

## A bug, or a change you want, in one of these repositories

Open an issue in that repository and choose the bug report or the feature request form. Say what you ran, what you expected and what happened instead. A pull request is welcome too; `CONTRIBUTING.md` says what review looks for.

## A security vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Report it privately to **security@trooth.co**, under the [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://trooth.co/security/vulnerability-disclosure-policy). `SECURITY.md` has the details.

## What a company's record shows

To claim your company's record, sign up at [trooth.co/signup](https://trooth.co/signup). To ask for a correction to what a record shows, follow the [Correction Policy](https://trooth.co/corrections). A GitHub issue is not the place for either, because it is public and Trooth cannot confirm there who you are.

## Privacy and legal

Privacy: **privacy@trooth.co**. Legal: **legal@trooth.co**.
